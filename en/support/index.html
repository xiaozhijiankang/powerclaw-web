Frequently asked questions

Can minors use PowerClaw?

PowerClaw is available to users aged fourteen and above. Users aged fourteen to seventeen must review the terms and Privacy Policy with a guardian, obtain their consent before using the service, and train under appropriate guidance. The service is not intended for children under fourteen.

How do I register and sign in?

Sign in with Apple is currently the only supported method, and sign-in is required to use the app. You do not need a separate email or phone account, a PowerClaw password, or additional profile information.

How do I download PowerClaw?

Use the App Store button on the homepage. If it shows “Not yet available”, the download link has not been enabled. Please check again later.

How do I use a training plan?

Enter your training goals, experience, schedule, and available equipment in the app to view a generated plan. Adjust it to your actual training circumstances. Specific controls depend on your app version.

Does it cost anything to use?

The service is currently free, including AI Q&A. Any future pricing changes will be explained before they take effect. Using the current free service will not automatically create a paid subscription.

Are workout records and AI conversations saved?

Yes. Workout records and AI conversation content are uploaded to and stored on Tencent Cloud servers in the Beijing region. See the Privacy Policy for information about personal information processing.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Me, select Delete Account, and follow the on-screen instructions. After you complete account deletion, the corresponding workout records, AI conversations, and related backups are immediately deleted from our servers. This page also has a Sign Out option. Signing out does not cancel your account.

For assistance or a personal information deletion request, contact support@powerclawapp.cn, explain your request, and provide an email address where you can receive a reply. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Can AI coach suggestions replace medical advice?

No. AI-assisted content provides general training references and may be inaccurate. If you have an injury, medical condition, or discomfort during training, stop the relevant exercise and consult a qualified medical professional.

Where can I find the terms and privacy policy?

Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Use the language selector at the top of each page to switch between Chinese and English. Both languages describe the same service.
