Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

PowerClaw’s official website and fitness training mobile application are operated by 北京贰零贰零科技发展有限公司 (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Information we process

1.1 Account information

PowerClaw requires sign-in and currently supports Sign in with Apple only. We use authentication information returned by Apple to identify your account and complete sign-in. We do not receive your Apple Account password. You do not need to create a separate PowerClaw password or fill in additional profile information during registration.

1.2 Workout records and AI conversations

When you use workout logging and AI Q&A, we process and store your workout records and AI conversation content to provide those services. Do not include personal information unrelated to your training question or other people’s private information in conversations.

1.3 Support information and website visits

When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide, to respond to and handle your request. Never send passwords or verification codes.

The website provides company and product information, policy documents, and support. It does not offer online registration, comments, or purchases. Its pages contain no third-party analytics scripts and do not use cookies or browser local storage to remember language selection.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate sign-in and provide account-related services.
  • Provide workout records, training plans, and AI-assisted Q&A.
  • Handle feedback, account cancellation, and personal information rights requests.
  • Maintain service security and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. Processing should be limited to what is needed to provide the services. If processing purposes, methods, or information categories change, we will provide notice and obtain renewed consent where required by law.

3. Third-party services

3.1 Sign in with Apple

We use Apple’s sign-in service for authentication. You can review authorisation information on Apple’s authorisation screen. See the Apple Privacy Policy for Apple’s processing practices.

3.2 Tencent Cloud

We use Tencent Cloud servers in the Beijing region to store workout records and AI conversations and provide related application API services.

3.3 Official Zhipu Open Platform

AI Q&A uses GLM-5.2 through the official Zhipu Open Platform API. Requests are sent to the platform for processing to generate responses. The platform is operated by 北京智谱华章科技股份有限公司. See its Privacy Policy and Service Agreement for relevant processing rules.

Where third-party services involve personal information, we will fulfil applicable notification, authorisation, and protection obligations. You may contact us using the details in this policy to learn more about the relevant processing.

4. Storage and retention

Workout records and AI conversations are stored on Tencent Cloud servers in the Beijing region. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for its processing purpose, unless otherwise required by law.

If personal information is provided outside mainland China, we will fulfil applicable notification, consent, and other legal obligations. The location of Tencent Cloud servers does not describe all processing locations of third parties such as Apple or Zhipu.

5. Account cancellation and data deletion

You can go to Me in the app, select Delete Account, and follow the on-screen instructions. After you complete account deletion, we immediately delete the corresponding workout records, AI conversations, and related backups from our servers. Sign Out is a separate action and does not cancel your account.

This deletion description applies to data held by us. Information independently processed by third parties is subject to their respective rules. Contact support@powerclawapp.cn for assistance with exercising relevant rights.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, a copy of, correction of, supplementation of, or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent-based authorisation, and cancel your account in accordance with law. In addition to the controls in the app, you can send requests to support@powerclawapp.cn.

To protect your account, we may need to verify identity information relevant to your request. We will handle and respond to requests within the period required by applicable law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

7. Protection of minors

PowerClaw is available to users aged fourteen and above. Users under eighteen must review this policy with a parent or other guardian and obtain their consent before using the service.

The service is not intended for children under fourteen. If a guardian discovers that a child under fourteen has used the service and provided personal information to us, they may contact support@powerclawapp.cn for assistance.

8. Information security

We will take security measures appropriate to our personal information processing activities to protect against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Internet services cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take remedial measures and fulfil applicable notification and reporting obligations.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy as services or legal requirements change. Changes affecting important personal information rights will be communicated appropriately, with renewed consent obtained where required by law.

10. Contact us
